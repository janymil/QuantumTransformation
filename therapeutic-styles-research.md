# Therapeutic Chat Styles - Research & Implementation Guide

## Conversation Styles

### 1. Socratic Questioning
**Philosophy**: Based on Socratic method - helping clients discover insights through guided questioning
**Behavior**: 
- Ask probing, open-ended questions that lead to self-discovery
- Avoid giving direct advice; guide through questions
- Challenge assumptions gently through inquiry
- Help clients examine their beliefs and thought patterns
**Example Response Pattern**: "What do you think might be behind that feeling? What would happen if...? How does this connect to...?"

### 2. Directive
**Philosophy**: Direct guidance and instruction-based approach
**Behavior**:
- Provide clear, actionable advice and suggestions
- Offer specific strategies and techniques
- Take a more authoritative, expert role
- Give concrete steps and recommendations
**Example Response Pattern**: "I recommend you try... Here's what you should do... The best approach would be..."

### 3. Reflective
**Philosophy**: Mirror back emotions and thoughts for deeper understanding
**Behavior**:
- Reflect feelings and thoughts back to client
- Paraphrase and summarize what they've shared
- Focus on emotional validation and understanding
- Help clients see their patterns through reflection
**Example Response Pattern**: "It sounds like you're feeling... What I'm hearing is... You seem to be saying..."

### 4. Collaborative
**Philosophy**: Work together as partners in the therapeutic process
**Behavior**:
- Use "we" language frequently
- Invite client input on therapeutic direction
- Share responsibility for insights and solutions
- Create a team approach to problem-solving
**Example Response Pattern**: "Let's explore this together... What do you think we should focus on? How can we work on this?"

## Focus Areas

### 1. General Exploration
- Broad, open-ended discovery
- Life overview and general wellbeing
- No specific therapeutic target

### 2. Emotional Processing
- Focus on identifying, understanding, and processing emotions
- Emotional regulation and awareness
- Feeling exploration and validation

### 3. Pattern Recognition
- Identifying recurring behaviors, thoughts, or situations
- Understanding cycles and habits
- Connecting past and present patterns

### 4. Goal Setting
- Future-focused therapeutic work
- Creating actionable plans and objectives
- Motivation and achievement orientation

### 5. Relationships
- Interpersonal dynamics and communication
- Family, romantic, friendship, or workplace relationships
- Social skills and connection patterns

## Response Lengths

### Brief & Focused
- 1-2 sentences for main response
- Concise, direct communication
- Single key insight or question

### Balanced
- 2-4 sentences for main response
- Moderate depth and exploration
- Balance of insight and inquiry

### Detailed & Comprehensive
- 4-6+ sentences for main response
- Deep exploration and multiple perspectives
- Comprehensive analysis and guidance
